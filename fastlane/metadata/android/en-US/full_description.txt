Mighty Calendar Alarms (redpantyhose.claude.mightycalendaralarm) turns your calendar
events into fullscreen alarms. It is a fork of BlackyHawky's Clock (based on AOSP),
released under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

The app has no internet permission and is 100% offline. Location access is only
used to read nearby Wi-Fi networks for the optional Wi-Fi condition of a profile.

Calendar alarms:
<ul>
  <li>Profiles pick events from one calendar by a title pattern (for example "Alarm:");</li>
  <li>Per profile: ringtone, volume (a share of the app's alarm volume), vibration, flashlight, escalation and maximum ring time;</li>
  <li>Optional alarm before the event, and optional alarm at the end of the event;</li>
  <li>Three snooze lengths on the alarm screen and in the notification, with optional extending of the event;</li>
  <li>Optional Wi-Fi condition: ring only near chosen networks;</li>
  <li>Lookahead of 7 to 30 days, checked continuously; tap a profile to see its upcoming alarms;</li>
  <li>Backup and restore of profiles and settings, optionally scheduled.</li>
</ul>

Also included:
<ul>
  <li>World clock with local time and cities, digital or analog style;</li>
  <li>Timer and multiple stopwatches;</li>
  <li>Widgets showing the next calendar alarm;</li>
  <li>Light, dark or system theme with AMOLED mode, and a customizable interface.</li>
</ul>
